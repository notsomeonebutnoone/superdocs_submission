# Workflow Modernization Proposal

**Prepared for:** Atlas Field Systems — Operations Team  
**Prepared by:** Northstar Workflow Studio  
**Date:** August 15, 2026  
**Status:** Draft for review

## Executive Summary

Atlas Field Systems is considering an update to its inspection-reporting workflow. The current manual reporting process delays handoff to the operations team. Northstar Workflow Studio proposes a focused six-week engagement consisting of a discovery workshop, reporting workflow configuration, and team enablement.

The engagement is structured to establish a shared understanding of the current process before configuration begins. Discovery findings will guide the workflow design, and the operations team will review the configured workflow before final handoff. Team enablement and a concise operating guide will support the transition at the end of the engagement.

This proposal intentionally avoids assumptions about systems, staffing, reporting volumes, compliance requirements, financial impact, or performance targets that Atlas Field Systems has not supplied. Those details will be confirmed during discovery where relevant. Pricing and legal terms remain unchanged from the approved template.

## Engagement Objectives

The engagement has four objectives:

- Document the current inspection-reporting and operations-handoff process.
- Identify the inputs, participants, review points, outputs, and decisions that shape that process.
- Configure an agreed reporting workflow based on validated discovery findings.
- Enable the operations team to understand and use the configured workflow at handoff.

These objectives define the direction of the work without pre-selecting a technical solution. Configuration decisions will be based on information confirmed with Atlas Field Systems during the engagement.

## Current State

Atlas Field Systems currently prepares inspection reports through a manual process. That process delays handoff to the operations team. The delay is the only confirmed current-state problem assumed by this proposal. No additional claims about cost, compliance, error rates, reporting volume, customer impact, or staffing have been supplied.

Because the present workflow has not yet been documented in detail, the engagement begins with discovery rather than immediate configuration. The discovery workshop will establish how an inspection report moves from initial input through preparation, review, completion, and operational handoff. It will also identify the participants responsible for each confirmed step and the information required at each transition.

The purpose of this current-state review is to ensure the configured workflow reflects the way Atlas Field Systems needs to operate and that unresolved questions are visible before configuration choices become difficult to change.

## Proposed Approach

Northstar Workflow Studio will deliver the engagement in three connected phases. Each phase produces an input for the next: discovery establishes the requirements, configuration turns agreed requirements into a working flow, and enablement prepares the operations team for handoff.

### Phase 1: Discovery workshop

The discovery phase will establish a shared, evidence-based view of the existing inspection-reporting workflow. Northstar Workflow Studio will facilitate a structured workshop with the Atlas Field Systems operations team and any participants Atlas Field Systems identifies as necessary.

Workshop topics will include:

- The starting point and intended outcome of an inspection report.
- The information required to prepare a report.
- The participants involved in preparation, review, approval, and handoff.
- The order of confirmed workflow steps.
- Existing review points and decision boundaries.
- The expected output at operational handoff.
- Known exceptions or unresolved questions identified by Atlas Field Systems.

Following the workshop, Northstar Workflow Studio will organize the confirmed findings into a current-state workflow summary and a configuration requirements list. Atlas Field Systems will review those materials before configuration proceeds.

### Phase 2: Reporting workflow configuration

Northstar Workflow Studio will configure the reporting workflow using the requirements confirmed during discovery. Configuration will focus on the agreed inspection-reporting and operations-handoff sequence and will not extend into unrelated business processes.

Configuration activities will include:

- Translating approved requirements into workflow steps.
- Representing the confirmed inputs and outputs at the appropriate stages.
- Establishing the agreed review and handoff points.
- Preparing a reviewable version of the configured workflow.
- Recording open items that require an Atlas Field Systems decision.
- Refining the configuration in response to consolidated review feedback.

The operations team will review the configured workflow before it is treated as ready for handoff. A workflow is not considered accepted merely because it has been configured; acceptance requires confirmation against the agreed requirements.

### Phase 3: Team enablement and handoff

The final phase will prepare the operations team to understand the configured workflow and its intended use. Northstar Workflow Studio will conduct a guided walkthrough and provide concise supporting documentation.

Enablement activities will include:

- A walkthrough of the configured reporting workflow.
- An explanation of the confirmed inputs, review points, and handoff steps.
- Guidance on the operating sequence captured during the engagement.
- A concise operating guide reflecting the final configuration.
- A final handoff session for questions and confirmation.

The enablement phase supports a clear operational handoff. It does not assume a broader training program, organization-wide rollout, or ongoing managed service unless separately approved in writing.

## Deliverables

### 1. Current-state workflow summary

A structured summary of the inspection-reporting and handoff process confirmed during discovery. It will identify the agreed participants, inputs, steps, review points, outputs, and open questions.

### 2. Configuration requirements list

A reviewable list of requirements derived from approved discovery findings. This list will provide the boundary for workflow configuration and later acceptance review.

### 3. Configured reporting workflow

A configured representation of the agreed inspection-reporting workflow, including the confirmed operational handoff steps and review points.

### 4. Review and decision log

A concise record of material configuration questions, feedback, and decisions made during the engagement. The log will make the basis for the final configuration understandable at handoff.

### 5. Operating guide

A concise guide describing the final workflow sequence, required inputs, review points, outputs, and handoff responsibilities as confirmed during the engagement.

### 6. Team walkthrough and final handoff

A guided review of the final configuration and operating guide, followed by confirmation of the completed deliverables.

## Working Principles

- **Evidence before configuration:** workflow decisions will be based on information confirmed during discovery.
- **Explicit boundaries:** unconfirmed requirements will be recorded as questions rather than treated as facts.
- **Review before acceptance:** Atlas Field Systems will review material outputs before final handoff.
